{{- /* --------------------------------------------------------------------- seo.html — title, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph, and the X card (#30, docs/prd/05-templates.md §"seo.html"). 1. ROBOTS: noindex,nofollow everywhere but production (the "preview" environment, netlify.toml), and on any page with `noindex: true`. 2. CANONICAL is always the production host, built on params.canonicalBaseURL: previews build with -b $DEPLOY_PRIME_URL, so .Permalink there is the *.netlify.app address. og:url deliberately stays .Permalink — Facebook re-scrapes whatever og:url names, so a production og:url would make the Sharing Debugger show the legacy site instead of the preview under test. In production the two match. 3. SHARE IMAGE: the page's own photo (`cover` on blog posts, `hero_image` on program pages, `photo.id` on team bios), else params.ogImage, cropped to 1200×630. Bio photos crop with g_faces: a tight family shot centre-cropped to 1.9:1 can lose the tallest head. Every other photo crops with g_auto, Cloudinary's content-aware crop: a centre crop of the portrait /ugo/ hero kept the box and cut off the face of the woman holding it. f_jpg, not the spec's f_auto: f_auto follows the requester's Accept header, and a crawler advertising WebP or AVIF would cache a format some share surfaces don't render. X reads og:title, og:description, and og:image, so twitter:card is the only twitter: tag needed. --------------------------------------------------------------------- */ -}} {{- /* One title for the tab and the share cards: on a Facebook card a bare "Give" says nothing about whose page it is. */ -}} {{- $title := cond .IsHome site.Title (printf "%s · %s" .Title site.Title) -}} {{- $description := or .Description site.Params.description -}} {{- $canonical := urls.JoinPath site.Params.canonicalBaseURL .RelPermalink -}} {{- $bioPhoto := and .Params.photo .Params.photo.id -}} {{- $image := or .Params.cover .Params.hero_image $bioPhoto site.Params.ogImage -}} {{- $gravity := cond (eq $image $bioPhoto) "g_faces," "g_auto," -}} {{- $ogImage := printf "%s/image/upload/c_fill,%sf_jpg,q_auto,w_1200,h_630/%s" site.Params.cloudinaryBase $gravity $image -}} {{ $title }} {{- if and hugo.IsProduction (not .Params.noindex) }} {{- else }} {{- end }} {{/* Open Graph */}}
