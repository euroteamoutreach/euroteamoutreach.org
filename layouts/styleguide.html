Inline links

A link inside running text on a light ground: semibold house blue, underlined, ringed on focus. On hover the underline drops away, a change the eye catches where a darker blue alone did not. The underline is what marks it as a link; colour alone reads as emphasis. Use .link on an <a> the template writes, and .prose-links on a block of rendered Markdown. Nav, cards, buttons and the dark footer keep their own link faces.

{{/* Standalone links: the `link-standalone` utility in main.css. */}}

Standalone links

A link on its own line or beside a card, not inside a sentence: a bio's back link, "Back to top", a card's second link. The reverse of an inline link: it rests bare, since its position, arrow and colour already mark it, and the underline appears on hover. The rule for both roles: inline links rest underlined, standalone links rest bare, and hover flips the underline. Use .link-standalone; size stays with the element.

Linked cards

A card whose whole face is one link. At rest, a 1px warm-200 rule; on hover or keyboard focus, a blue border and three blue rings that read as a glow — rings, not a shadow, so nothing lifts off the page. Blue, never gold. Put .linked-card on the card and .stretched-link on the <a> in its heading: the link stretches over the card, and a screen reader names it by the heading. The ground and padding are the caller's. Hover or tab to see it.

The four surfaces

A closed set. Stacked here so the section rhythm reads as rhythm rather than as a list of values. Separation is a change of ground, a 1px warm-200 rule, or space — never a shadow.

paper — the default ground

Most content sits here. Warm, low-contrast, and quiet enough that a filled gold button is the loudest thing on the page.

white — cards, raised panels, the header

Reads as lifted against paper without needing a shadow. The header sits here; warmth is carried by elements further down the page.

eto-50 — quiet emphasis, the doorway band

A tint of the brand, not a second brand colour. Marks a band as distinct without competing with a giving CTA.

eto-900 — the footer and stat bands

The only heavy surface, and the one place gold is legible as text.

Photos

Photographs come from Cloudinary through cloudinary-img.html: a responsive srcset at five widths, each c_limit,f_auto,q_auto, so the browser picks the size and Cloudinary picks the format and compression. Lazy by default; alt is required, and a missing one fails the build. Corners are rounded-eto.
