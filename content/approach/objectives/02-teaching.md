"So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God."
{{< /scripture >}}

Because we believe the Bible is the greatest evangelistic weapon in our arsenal, we work to get as many people as possible into the Scriptures for themselves. Our primary means is _Bible First_, the study course we wrote and have refined over the years. Its aim isn't only to teach the Bible as information; it's to draw a person closer to God through His own Word — trusting that someone who reads the Scriptures honestly is being drawn toward the most important decision of all, to follow Christ. The same conviction shapes the rest of our teaching: Bible studies in homes as the opportunity comes, and steady teaching in our local church — at present, a verse-by-verse course through the book of Romans.
