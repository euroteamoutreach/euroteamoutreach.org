{{< scripture ref="Mark 16:15" >}}
"And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature."
{{< /scripture >}}

This is the heart of it: telling people, plainly, that Jesus Christ died for sinners and rose again, and calls everyone to repent and believe. It has taken many forms over the years — open-air preaching with chalk drawings in a city square, Gospel films shown in mountain villages, a _Good and Evil_ book handed across a table, an unhurried conversation on a street corner. Today it looks like preaching to families gathered in the de-occupied villages of the south, where a box of food opens the door to the message that matters most. The venue changes; the news does not.
