{{< scripture ref="2 Timothy 2:2" >}}
"And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also."
{{< /scripture >}}

Our desire is to pass on to others what God has taught us — not at arm's length, but by sharing life. For most of our history this took the form of **Carpathian Mountain Outreach**: each summer we brought a team to Ukraine to learn the work of a missionary by living it alongside us. We studied the language together, camped together, learned to shop and find our way in a new culture, talked through whatever was going on in each other's lives, and became friends. The missionary tasks mattered — but so did the years of friendship they grew into. That same heart carries on through our local church today and, we pray, through UGO as it grows into a path for the next generation to take up the work first-hand.
