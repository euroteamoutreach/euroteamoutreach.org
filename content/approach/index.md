Euro Team Outreach exists to fulfill the Great Commission of our Lord Jesus Christ, who told His disciples, _"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations…"_ (Matthew 28:19).

Everything we do falls under one of three objectives — three ways of obeying that one command. They aren't separate departments; they're three angles on a single work.
